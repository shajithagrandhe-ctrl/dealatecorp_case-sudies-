MediStock

Offline-first pharmacy inventory and billing application

MediStock is a pharmacy workspace for medicines, stock, purchases, billing, branches, reports and local operational control.

01

Problem Statements

01

Inventory accuracy

Medicine inventory needs to stay accurate, searchable and connected to batch, expiry and branch details.

02

Low-stock risk

Low-stock conditions need clear visibility before they disrupt sales or daily pharmacy work.

03

Disconnected workflows

Billing, invoices, purchases, suppliers and branches are connected workflows that can become hard to manage separately.

04

Connectivity dependency

Core pharmacy operations should remain usable when internet connectivity is unreliable.

02

Solutions

01

Local pharmacy workspace

Medicines, stock, purchases and sales are organized inside one structured operational app.

02

Live stock visibility

Dashboard and stock screens surface medicine counts, branch availability, pricing, expiry and low-quantity alerts.

03

Integrated billing

Sales billing captures patient details, bill items, tax, discounts, payment method and refill scheduling.

04

Offline-first persistence

SQLite keeps core pharmacy workflows available locally without depending on a web backend for daily operation.

03

Tech Stack

Flutter Dart SQLite AppController AppDatabase PDF / CSV Export
